# Character art provenance

Active hero: VOLT, a palette variant of Quaternius's Punk model from the Ultimate Modular Men Pack.

- Artist and authoritative pack/license page: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
- License: CC0 1.0. Local notice: LICENSE.txt.
- Download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase/main/public/glb/modular_men/Punk.glb
- Mirror's license inventory: https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase/blob/main/LICENSES.md
- The official Drive download returned quota-exceeded HTML; no such response is treated as an asset.
- The mirror contains the skinned model but no animation clips. RIFTOPS currently supplies procedural idle, arm posing and locomotion through scripts/art/operator.gd. This is not a complete authored animation set.
- RIFTOPS changes: teal/navy/orange material palette; normalization; skeletal pose adapter; first-person-axis orientation; lobby presentation.
- Swat.glb is retained as an evaluated alternative from the same pack/mirror, not a selectable or completed second hero.
- All assets are bundled locally. No runtime downloads or internet dependency.

These are licensed reusable models, not models extracted from FRAG, T3 Arena or Ace Force. Their character designs are not claimed as original RIFTOPS geometry.
