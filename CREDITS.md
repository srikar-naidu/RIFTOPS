  recordings used for the seven weapons
- Kenney's [Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds, and
  Music Jingles](https://kenney.nl/assets) — UI, foley, effects, and round

  [`assets/audio/LICENSE_Kenney_CC0.txt`](assets/audio/LICENSE_Kenney_CC0.txt)
- [Fire Crackling](https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling) by
  AntumDeluge — source for `fire_loop.ogg`
- [100 CC0 SFX #2](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2) by
  rubberduck — source material for the fountain and generator loops
- [30 CC0 SFX loops](https://opengameart.org/content/30-cc0-sfx-loops) by
  rubberduck — source material for the town ambience bed
- [wind1](https://opengameart.org/content/wind1) by Luke.RUSTLTD — public
  domain source material for the two wind loops

The ambient source recordings were trimmed, filtered, resampled, and made
seamless for use in Valu. The synthesized spike beeps are project-created.

See [`docs/ASSETS.md`](docs/ASSETS.md) for every bundled filename, processing
note, and in-game use.
