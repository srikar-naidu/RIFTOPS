Valu is an original tactical first-person shooter built for team play on a local network.

Host a match or join another device on the same Wi-Fi or LAN. Fight in objective-based rounds or team deathmatch, choose from distinct combat roles, buy weapons and equipment, and use tactical abilities. Touch controls are designed for landscape phones.

Multiplayer is LAN-only: there is no online matchmaking or hosted service. Valu has no ads, trackers, analytics, accounts, or in-app purchases.
